# Release and legal preparation checklist

Before commercial launch, clear the final product name and logo in relevant countries and trademark classes; secure rights to every film, series, music, poster, trailer, subtitle, font, and illustration; publish privacy, terms, cookie/consent, subscription, refund/cancellation, and moderation policies; implement age/content controls; complete security and accessibility reviews; configure a production database, TLS, secret management, backups, logging, monitoring, incident response and data-retention/deletion processes.

The prototype's sample video uses a CC0 sample clip; it is not a content delivery implementation. Demo membership endpoints do not charge money. The admin bootstrap requires environment variables. The default token secret is for local development only.

Patent protection is not automatic for a UI or business concept. Consult qualified IP counsel about patentability, trademark clearance, design rights, and freedom-to-operate in launch markets. This repository makes no legal-clearance guarantee.
